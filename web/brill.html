The double-dummy score is the session scored with Rusty's contracts all played double dummy (and everyone else's): what the bidding earned, whoever played the cards. The actual score is the session as played (BEN plays Rusty's cards); the cardplay is the difference. IMP sessions are totals over the session's 8 boards (0 = the field's average), MP sessions percentages (50% = average). A dashed step is the mean over a deployment period.
