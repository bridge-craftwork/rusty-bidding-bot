# rkcb-1430 (`rkcb-1430.bid`): notes

Roman Keycard Blackwood, 1430 answers, keyed off auction state (agreed
trump, game force) rather than auction patterns. Cases: `rkcb-1430.test`.

## Guidance

- Keycards are tracked with the **five-keycard limit**: partner's answer is
  combined with my own count, so "1 or 4" facing my two is 1.
- **When the answer stays ambiguous**, the asker signs off in five of the
  trump suit, and the answerer corrects to six holding the higher count.
- Never pass a keycard answer (unless it is five of our suit, a sign-off):
  that is a mistake, not a judgment difference.

## Evidence from BBA

Texas_Transfer (500 boards), after 4NT:

- `5C` then BBA's 5D is a queen ask (4 boards); we bid six directly.
- `5S` (two and the queen) then BBA bids 7S with 15–20 (5 boards); we bid 6S.
- `5D` ("0 or 3") then BBA bids six with 13–16, reading it as 3 (9 boards).

## Accepted differences from BBA

- The ambiguous "0 or 3": BBA assumes the count that fits; we sign off in
  five and let partner correct (Rick's rule).

## When to ask

The two-branch test in the `# The ask` block is shared with
`blackwood.bid` and is written up once in **`slam-catch.notes.md`**. It
is Rick's rule: **33 combined support points** with the agreed suit as
trump (`we.tp(trump)`), read against partner's maximum when partner is
limited and against partner's floor in a game force, plus at most four
losers and **no two bare side suits**.

It replaced `when slam_try` (31+ combined HCP) and, with it, the
`we.forcing = game` requirement in the ask context: the module also
serves auctions that merely agree a trump suit, which is where the drops
were.

On the 21GF cards, which have `Cue bid = 1`, a hand with **one** bare
side suit does not come here at all: it control-bids first
(`control-bids.bid`) and returns to 4NT only once partner has shown a
control above it. That is the third rule at priority 5, gated on
`asked control(t), answered control(u)` — partner control-bid *and* was
answering mine, so I am the hand that opened the dialogue and the one
that takes charge. Removing it costs 0.9 points of contract agreement
and 830 IMPs on the slam scenarios, so it is the part of the dialogue
that earns its keep.
