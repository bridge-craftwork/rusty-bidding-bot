# Flannery 2♦: notes

## Decisions

- Opening: exactly 4-5 in the majors, 11-15 HCP, any seat, ahead of 1♥
  and 1NT (priority 1). The weak 2♦ is off (preempts.bid, `weak_2d_open`).
- Responder's weak calls follow BBA (Flannery scenario): pass with six
  diamonds and at most two hearts, 2♥ with up to 10 HCP and no better
  call, 2♠ with four spades (or three and a singleton heart). 2NT from 11.
- Game in a major goes through the 4♣/4♦ transfer; the direct 4♥/4♠ of
  Bridge Bum are not written.
- Over a double the same responses (system on).
- After the 2NT answer the asker bids 3NT when the short suit is covered
  (his stopper or opener's doubleton), else 4♥ in the 5-2 fit.
