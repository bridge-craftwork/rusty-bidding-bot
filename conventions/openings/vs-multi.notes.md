# Defence to a Multi 2♦: notes

## Decisions

- Their 2♦ is recognised by what their card made it show: six hearts and
  six spades both possible, and diamonds possibly short. That holds for a
  Multi, and also for any other artificial 2♦ that shows no suit
  (Benjamin's game force, for example); not for a weak 2♦ or Flannery.
- Direct seat (Bridge Bum, option 2): X 13-15 balanced or 16+; 2♥/2♠
  natural 10-15; 2NT 16-18 balanced; 3m natural six+; 3M six+, 13-16.
  Anything else passes (priority 1: above the defence to a natural 2♦).
- Fourth seat after (2♦) P (2♥/2♠): X takeout of the suit bid (13+),
  natural suits, 2NT 15-18. Leaping Michaels 4m is not written.
- Advancer's calls are left to the general competitive modules: they
  read the double as 13+.

## BBA (compare Opps_Multi_2D --limit 50, 2026-10-05: 78% of calls agree)

- BBA's direct 3♦ (a few boards where we double or bid 2NT) and its
  passes with a five-card heart suit and 10-11 HCP: BBA style.
