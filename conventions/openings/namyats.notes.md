# Namyats: notes

## Decisions

- 4♣ = strong 4♥ preempt, 4♦ = strong 4♠ preempt (Bridge Bum). The
  hand is BBA's: exactly seven cards headed by the ace-king-queen, 9-13
  HCP, no side ace or king; seats 1-3, ahead of the one-level opening.
  The plain 4♥/4♠ preempt (preempts.bid) is unchanged.
- Why BBA's hand and not Bridge Bum's "8-9 tricks": the first version
  (seven+ good cards, five losers, 10-15 HCP) changed ~70 boards outside
  the Namyats scenario where BBA (21GF-GIB, which has Namyats on) opened
  1M. In the whole corpus BBA opens 4♣/4♦ nine times, every time with
  the hand above; with eight such cards and 9-10 HCP it opens 4M, with a
  side ace or king (12-13 HCP) 1M.
- Responder: the next step is a slam-interest relay (five controls, or
  15+ HCP, with three quick tricks); the major signs off. A keycard 4NT
  straight away is also offered by the slam modules (trump is known).
- After the relay opener shows a side singleton or void, else returns to
  the major. Bridge Bum leaves the rebids to agreement; shortness-showing
  is our choice.

## BBA

- The Namyats scenario plays 21GF-SPECIALS, which has `Namyats = 0`, so
  the scenario does not exercise the convention. Probes with
  21GF-PolishTwoSuiters (probes/namyats-openings.toml, -3rd): no 4♣/4♦
  on eight- or seven-card hands with side honours, as above.
- BBA's continuations after 4♣/4♦ in the corpus: responder signs off in
  the major every time.
