# French 2♦: notes

## Decisions

- The convention-card entry leaves the meaning to the partnership. We
  take the French standard (SEF) meaning, which is also BBA's `French 2D`
  (probes/b2-French-openings.toml, b2-French-openings-strong.toml): 2♦ is
  the artificial game force, 24+ HCP of any shape, balanced 26-27
  included (BBA opens 2♦, not 3NT). 2♣ stays the strong opening of
  strong-openings.bid, capped at 23.
- Responses are BBA's ace answers (probes/b2-French-resp-2D*.toml):
  2♥ no ace 0-7, 2♠ one major ace, 2NT no ace 8+, 3♣/3♦ the ace of that
  suit, 3♥/3♠ no ace 0-7 and a five-card suit, 3NT two aces or more.
  BBA bids 2♥ with a long minor and no ace; so do we.
- Opener rebids naturally (five-card suits, notrump balanced); over 3♥/3♠
  he agrees with three; over 3NT a balanced 25+ bids 6NT.
- The weak 2♦ is off (preempts.bid). Responses to 2♣ are unchanged:
  BBA's French 2♣ (19-24 by its alert) answers 2♦ waiting, and our ladder
  already does.

## Open questions (decision taken)

- After 2♠ (a major ace) BBA may ask which; we let opener bid naturally.
- No interference structure over 2♦.
- 2♣–2♦–2NT is passable in BBA's French scheme; ours stays game forcing
  as strong-openings.bid plays it.
