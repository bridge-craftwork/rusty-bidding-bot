# Polish two-suiters: notes

## Decisions

- 2♥ = five hearts and another five-card suit (spades possible), 2♠ =
  five spades and a five-card minor; 5-11 HCP, seats 1-3, ahead of the
  one-level opening (BBA: 5 HCP opens, 12 opens 1♥;
  probes/polish-openings.toml). Wikipedia says 6-11.
- No weak twos in the majors with this on (preempts.bid); BBA passes a
  weak six-card major (probe).
- Responder (BBA, probes/polish-resp-2H.toml, -2S.toml): pass with
  three hearts / two spades (BBA passes 2♥ even with 13 HCP and three
  hearts); with a misfit 2♠ (over 2♥) or 3♣ (over 2♠) pass-or-correct;
  2NT asks from 15. Wikipedia: over 2♥, 2♠ is pass or correct and 2NT
  asks (3♥ = spades, others natural).
- The 3♥/3♠ preemptive raises and the asker's placements are ours.

## Partial

- No scenario plays these openings as such (Benjamin_2D's card turns
  them on, but its hands are strong). Interference beyond a double, and
  opener's strength after the 2NT ask, are not written.

## Open questions

- Does the 2NT ask also want opener's strength (minimum/maximum)? Not
  in our source; not written.
