# gazzilli (`gazzilli.bid`): notes

Gazzilli after 1♥/1♠ - 1NT and 1♥ - 1♠. Partial (2026-10-05). Cases:
`gazzilli.test`.

## What the card says

`major_openings.gazzilli.play` (`.bbsa` `Gazzilli`; 21GF-Gazzilli sets it).

## The structure played

- Opener's 2♣: 11-15 with four or more clubs, or 16+ (classic form).
  Not 18-19 balanced (2NT at once, as Todd), and not six of the major
  with 16-18 (the jump rebid, as BBA). After 1♥-1♠ only without four
  spades: a fit raises. A minimum six-card one-suiter rebids 2M; 5-5
  with 13-15 jumps in the second suit (PBS summary, Todd).
- Responder: 2♦ with 8+ HCP; with less, 2M with two or more, else a
  five-card major of his own (2♥ over 1♠; 2♠ over 1♥-1♠), a six-card
  minor at the three level, or 2NT (no fit).
- After 2♦: 2M the natural minimum with clubs (not forcing); 2NT 16-17
  balanced; 3NT 20-21 balanced; 3♣/3♦ 16+ with the minor, 3M 16+ with
  six, the other major 16+ with four, 2♠ after 1♥-1♠ with three spades
  (all game forcing); otherwise 4M.
- Responder after the minimum: game with 12, invite with 10-11, 2NT/3NT
  without a fit (2♠ with five spades after 1♥-1♠), else pass. After a
  game force: 4M with three, else 3NT; opener corrects 3NT to 4M with six.
- Opener after a weak answer: pass up to 18; 3M with six and 19-20; game
  above.

## Deviations

- From Todd's "modern" Gazzilli: there the minimum six-card one-suiter
  goes through 2♣ and the minimum 5-4 with clubs rebids 2M. BBA plays the
  classic form (every 2♣ in the Gazzilli corpus holds clubs or 16+), and so
  do we. The PBS scenario's summary mixes the two.
- Not played: the artificial rebids after 2♦ (2oM "some four-card suit"
  with its relay, 2NT "six and a four-card side suit"), and any defence
  to interference (the convention is off in competition).
- BBA starts 18-19 balanced with 2♣ too (then 2NT); we bid 2NT at once.
