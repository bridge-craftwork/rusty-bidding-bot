# choice-of-games (`choice-of-games.bid`): notes

Opener's answer when responder's 3NT offers a choice of games: after
Stayman (responder has the other major), or after a transfer with exactly
five. Cases: `choice-of-games.test`.

## Guidance

- **Opener corrects 3NT to 4M with an eight-card fit**: four opposite four
  (after Stayman), or three opposite five (after a transfer). Otherwise
  pass.
