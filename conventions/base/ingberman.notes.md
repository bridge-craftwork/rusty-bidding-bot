# ingberman (`ingberman.bid`): notes

Ingberman 2NT (`other_conventions.ingberman_2nt.play`): after opener's
reverse (1x-1y-2z, 17+, forcing one round), responder's 2NT is weak
(up to 8 total points) and asks for 3♣. Cases: `ingberman.test`. No PBS
scenario, and no stock card switches it on.

## Structure

- A reverse is recognised as the two-level new suit that leaves the
  auction forcing one round (`we.forcing = round`): jump shifts force to
  game, lower new suits do not force.
- After 2NT-3♣ responder passes with clubs (opener's first suit, or five
  of them), signs off in three of opener's first suit, bids three of the
  reverse suit with four (invitational: opener passes a minimum reverse),
  or three of his own six-card suit.
- 2y (his own suit): five or more, forcing one round; opener raises with
  three, shows six of his first suit or five of the reverse suit, 3NT
  with 19+, else 2NT, which responder passes with 8 or less.
- 3x, 3z, 3y and 3NT: natural and game forcing, 9+.
- Off over interference (field definition is silent; Todd describes the
  uncontested reverse).

## Questions for Rick

- The weak threshold: Todd's "5 to 7-" points. We use up to 8 total
  points (opener's 17 plus 8 is game). Decision taken: 8.
- Opener always bids 3♣ over 2NT, even with a game-forcing reverse
  (19+); some play that opener may then bid on. Decision: always 3♣;
  responder's invitational 3z lets a strong opener bid game.
