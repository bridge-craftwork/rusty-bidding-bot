# strong-openings (`strong-openings.bid`): notes

2NT (20-21 balanced) and the artificial 2♣ (22+), with responder's waiting
2♦ and positives, and opener's description after 2♦. Cases:
`strong-openings.test`.

## Evidence

BBA's own meanings: 2NT 20-21 balanced, 2♣ "strong", 19+ by its note but
17-21 on the hands it held.

## Accepted differences from BBA

- BBA opens one of a suit with 20-21 balanced on many hands, where we open
  2NT.

## Gaps

- Responder's continuations after opener's rebid (Stayman and transfers
  over the 2NT rebid are keyed to a 1NT opening, so they do not apply).
- 2♣ after interference; the very_strong and natural card options.
