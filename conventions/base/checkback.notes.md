# checkback (`checkback.bid`): notes

Checkback Stayman, card field `other_conventions.checkback.play` (on in
21GF-GIB-Bergen and 21GF-PolishTwoSuiters, both without NMF). Cases:
`checkback.test`.

## Structure

- After 1x-1y-1NT, 2♣ is artificial, invitational or better (11+ total
  points): five of responder's major, or both majors; after 1♣-1♦-1NT, a
  four-card major. Ranked ahead of a natural 2NT or 3NT, as NMF.
- Opener: two of responder's major with three; after a 1♠ response four
  hearts (2♥); after 1♣-1♦ either four-card major; else 2♦.
- Responder: with a fit (5-3, 4-4) 3M invites and 4M is game; without one
  2NT invites and 3NT is game. Opener answers the invitations in
  rebids.bid.
- On, it switches off NMF and the natural 2♣ after 1NT
  (responder-rebids.bid).

## Open questions

- No minimum/maximum split in opener's answers (Bridge Bum has none at
  this step); some partnerships play 2♦ = minimum. Not built.
- No PBS scenario plays Checkback on the NS card, so there is no corpus
  check; cases only.

## Sources

- **Bridge Bum, "Checkback Stayman"**
  (https://www.bridgebum.com/checkback_stayman.php): 2♣ after the 1NT
  rebid, invitational or better; 2♦ denies, 2M shows three, the other
  major four.
- **Where we differ:** responder's 2♣ needs five of the major or both
  majors (a four-card major alone bids notrump); Bridge Bum allows any
  invitational hand. Standard practice, not yet cited.
