# wolff: Wolff sign-off after opener's 2NT rebid

Card field `other_conventions.wolff_signoff.play` (new, 2026-09-30; no
`.bbsa` key: BBA does not play it, so the corpus never turns it on).
After 1x-1y-2NT (18-19) responder's 3C asks opener to bid 3D; responder
passes with six diamonds or bids three of his major to play. Every other
call but pass forces to game (game-force.bid).

Built for weak hands only (0-6 points and a six-card diamond suit or
responder's major). The other use of 3C in the full convention (a slam or
game try through the relay, and 3D/3H/3S follow-ups showing shape) is not
built.

## Sources

- The Wolff sign-off (Bobby Wolff): standard practice, not yet cited; the
  weak-only subset built here is ours.
